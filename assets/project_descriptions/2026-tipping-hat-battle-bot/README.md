# Tipping Hat Battle Bot

- **Year:** 2026
- **Context:** ME 444 · Battle Bot Competition

## Summary

Developed a hat-inspired lifting mechanism and integrated it with a compact drive system for a remotely controlled battle bot. The design packaged the mechanism and supplied electronics within a 10-inch cube and 5-pound limit; the team earned first place in the ME 444 competition.

## Objective

Immobilize opposing robots while surviving field hazards under the course constraints.

## Design Constraints

- Fit within a 10 × 10 × 10 inch envelope.
- Stay at or below 5 pounds.
- Use the provided electronics.
- Package the lifting mechanism and drivetrain together.

## Engineering Work

Developed the lifting mechanism, compact drive system, and full CAD assembly described in the existing portfolio. Media include the hat-tip mechanism, drive system, Creo assembly, and final build.

## Outcome and Evidence

The existing portfolio records a team first-place award. Final-build and mechanism images document the project.
