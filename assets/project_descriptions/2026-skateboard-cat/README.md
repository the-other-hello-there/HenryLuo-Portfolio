# Felon the Freestylin’ Feline

- **Year:** 2026
- **Context:** ME 444 · Interactive Toy Design

## Summary

Contributed heavily to an interactive skateboard cat, including a split chassis that separates aesthetic pieces from functional components. The architecture supports easier 3D printing, revision, and assembly while integrating the toy’s electromechanical systems; the team earned an Honorable Mention.

## Objective

Create an interactive skateboard cat with at least two nontrivial electromechanical systems.

## Design Constraints

- Fit within a 10 × 10 × 10 inch envelope.
- Work within a $70 budget.
- Integrate electromechanical systems while supporting fast revision and assembly.

## Engineering Work

Developed the split chassis described in the existing portfolio. Associated media cover the drive-system CAD, shell-securing assembly, complete Creo assembly, and final build.

## Outcome and Evidence

The existing portfolio records a team Honorable Mention in the ME 444 Interactive Toy project. A final-build photo accompanies the CAD images.
