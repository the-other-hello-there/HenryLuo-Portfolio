Overview:
The Display Hooks are a structural design intended to showcase hookable memorabilia. They provide an efficient and aesthetically pleasing way to display items.

Design Parameters:
• 3D-printed
  - Fits on print bed
  - Minimal supports
• Has hooks
• Minimal material usage
• Aesthetically pleasing (or artistically different)

Components:
• Display Hooks - standalone structure to hold 6 items and an optional item in the middle
