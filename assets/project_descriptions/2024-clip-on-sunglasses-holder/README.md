# Clip-On Sunglasses Holder

- **Year:** 2024
- **Context:** Personal project

## Summary

Designed a modular, 3D-printed holder for clip-on sunglasses that attaches to a car door pocket. Separate clip and housing components support accessible storage, while the housing can attach to the clip or another housing unit.

## Objective

Keep clip-on sunglasses accessible and securely stored in a car.

## Design Constraints

- Clip onto the car door pocket.
- Retain clip-on sunglasses.
- Fit printed parts on the print bed with minimal supports.

## Engineering Work

Developed separate attachment and storage components, with CAD and photos organized across multiple iterations.

## Outcome and Evidence

Versioned CAD and installed-prototype photos document iteration; retention and durability test results are not recorded.
