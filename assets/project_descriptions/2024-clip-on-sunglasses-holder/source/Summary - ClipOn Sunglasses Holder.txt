Overview:
The Clip-On Sunglasses Holder is a practical accessory for securely holding clip-on sunglasses in a car. Its simple design offers convenience and accessibility for glasses-wearing drivers.

Design Parameters:
• 3D-printed
  - Fits on print bed
  - Minimal supports
• Can hold clip-on sunglasses
• Can clip onto the door pocket of a car

Components:
• Clip - attaches to the car door pocket
• Housing - holds the clip-on sunglasses and can clip onto the clip or another housing unit
