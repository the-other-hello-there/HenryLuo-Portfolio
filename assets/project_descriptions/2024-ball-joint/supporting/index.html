    Overview:
This 3D-printed ball joint assembly was created to explore control mechanisms for humanoid robotic calves. Its design supports mobility and range of motion, serving as a foundational element for future prototypes.

Design Parameters:
• 3D-printed
  - Fits on print bed
  - Minimal supports
• Mimics ankle rotation
• Variable-based CADing to make it easy to make dimensional adjustments

Components:
• Ball - attaches to the calf and rotates in the socket
• Socket - houses the ball and attaches to the foot

    # Ball Joint

## Purpose
Create a ball-and-socket jig that simulates ankle rolling motion for the development of ankle controls.

## Requirements and constraints
- Provide ankle-like rotation.
- Use variable-based CAD for dimensional adjustments.
- Fit printed parts on the print bed with minimal supports.
- Keep assembly simple and retain the ball in the socket during use.

## Engineering contribution
Modeled the ball, socket, and assembly in SOLIDWORKS and produced a printed joint prototype.

## Outcome and limits
No separate quantified validation record is retained.
