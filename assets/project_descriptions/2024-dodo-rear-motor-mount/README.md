# Dodo Rear Motor Mount

- **Year:** 2024
- **Context:** PART · R&D Mechanical Systems

## Summary

Redesigned the Dodo UAV rear motor mount to clamp around the composite tail boom without a through-bolt in the boom. A split printed mount, butyl rubber contact strips, and four bolt/locknut pairs provide motor retention while addressing delamination concerns and preserving the existing aircraft structure.

## Objective

Secure the rear motor without modifying the composite tail boom.

## Design Constraints

- Clamp securely around the existing boom.
- Retain the motor and integrate with the aircraft.
- Use mostly printed parts sized for the print bed with minimal supports.
- Account for mount strength when limiting bolt preload and contact force.

## Engineering Work

Iterated a two-piece clamping mount with butyl rubber strips for compliant, high-friction contact. The mount material limits allowable clamping loads; the original notes identify compression and shear from bolt tension as design considerations.

## Outcome and Evidence

Versioned CAD, printed prototypes, final assembly photos, a design-review presentation, and a hover video are available. The supplied summary does not quantify clamp-load margins or delamination testing.
