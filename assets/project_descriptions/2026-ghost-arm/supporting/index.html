    # Ghost Arm

## Purpose
Develop a tele-operated robotic arm in 36 hours to improve manufacturing and maintenance ease and safety in dark, dirty, and difficult environments.

## Requirements and constraints
- 36-hour development window
- Only one attempt available to 3D-print the complete mechanical system
- CAD relation verification before fabrication

## Engineering contribution
I led purpose ideation and low-fidelity prototyping with cardboard and tape, helping define the robotic arm’s purpose and explore the concept before fabrication. I also worked on the 3D-printed mechanical system.

## Outcome and limits
A CAD relation error caused my fabrication attempt to fail, and the single-attempt constraint left no opportunity for a corrected print. This highlighted the importance of checking CAD relations and planning for fabrication risk. The team earned Best Implementation of Viam at StarkHacks 2026.

## Design process

The concept evolved rapidly during the short project timeline, so CAD did not meaningfully carry through to the final design. Physical prototypes and demonstration footage document the final implementation.
