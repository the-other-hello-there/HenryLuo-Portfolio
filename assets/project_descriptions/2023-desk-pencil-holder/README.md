# Desk Pencil Holder

- **Year:** 2023
- **Context:** Personal project

## Summary

Designed a 3D-printed pencil holder that hangs from a desk edge to keep writing utensils accessible while freeing desktop space. The compact geometry was developed around print-bed limits and minimal support material.

## Objective

Store writing utensils at the edge of a desk.

## Design Constraints

- Attach to the edge of a desk or table.
- Fit on the print bed with minimal supports.

## Engineering Work

Modeled a compact holder with desk-edge attachment geometry in Fusion 360.

## Outcome and Evidence

A CAD model and project image are available; load and fit test results are not recorded.
