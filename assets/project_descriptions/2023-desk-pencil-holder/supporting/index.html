    # Desk Pencil Holder

## Purpose
Store writing utensils at the edge of a desk.

## Requirements and constraints
- Attach to the edge of a desk or table.
- Fit on the print bed with minimal supports.

## Engineering contribution
Modeled a compact holder with desk-edge attachment geometry in Fusion 360.

## Outcome and limits
No separate quantified validation record is retained.
